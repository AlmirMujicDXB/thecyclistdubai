Disclaimer & data sources

How TheCyclistDubai Weather works, where the data comes from, and what to keep in mind before you ride.

⚠️ Disclaimer

This page is a free planning aid for cyclists. Forecasts are computer-model predictions and can be wrong, especially for wind gusts, fog, dust and sudden storms. Conditions on the track can differ from the forecast.

  • Always check the official UAE forecast and warnings from the National Center of Meteorology (NCM) before riding.
  • Dates more than 16 days ahead show typical weather from the same day in an earlier year, not a forecast. Past dates show recorded weather from the Open-Meteo historical archive.
  • The ride score, kit tips and the suggested 06:30–10:00 riding window are simple guides worked out from the forecast, not safety advice.
  • Follow track rules, opening hours and speed limits set by RTA, Dubai Municipality and Hatta Wadi Hub.
  • Ride within your ability, carry water and lights, and tell someone your plans for desert and mountain rides.

TheCyclistDubai accepts no responsibility for any loss, injury or damage arising from use of this information.

🌦️ Weather data

Temperature, feels-like, wind speed, gusts, direction, humidity, UV, rain chance, sunrise and sunset come live from Open-Meteo. It combines weather models from national weather services, including ECMWF, NOAA GFS, DWD ICON and Météo-France, at 1–11 km resolution. Data refreshes every 15 minutes and when you come back to the page.

Values can differ slightly from the official NCM forecast. Wind is measured at 10 m above ground; at rider height it is usually a little lower.

🗺️ Maps & tracks

  • Track lines are loaded live from OpenStreetMap cycleway and MTB trail data, © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Satellite imagery © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics.
  • Cycling map © CyclOSM / OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Colours on the track show the wind for your riding direction (set it with the direction button on the map): red is headwind, green is tailwind and yellow is crosswind. Small arrows on the track show the direction used. Meydan defaults to its official one-way anticlockwise direction.

🚴 Track information

  • Al Qudra Cycle Track: desert loops of 18, 49, 86 and 100+ km.
  • Meydan · Nad Al Sheba Cycle Park: 10 km loop, one-way anticlockwise.
  • Mushrif National Park: 50 km mountain-bike trails (Dubai Municipality).
  • Kite Beach · Jumeirah Beach Track: 16 km beachfront track (RTA).
  • Hatta MTB Trail Centre: ~53 km of graded trails from Hatta Wadi Hub.

All tracks have free showers and toilets, coffee shops and bike rental (Wolfi's) nearby.

Lengths and details are taken from RTA, Dubai Municipality, Visit Hatta and published guides, and may change.

🔒 Privacy

No account, no tracking. Your chosen track, units and map view are saved only in your own browser so the page remembers them next time. Screenshots for WhatsApp are made on your device and never uploaded by this site.
